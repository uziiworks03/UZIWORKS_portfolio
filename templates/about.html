ABOUT UZIWORKS

JOHN
MICHAEL

John Michael is a visual creative from the Philippines specializing in photography, videography, and video editing. His creative work is presented under uziworks, a personal creative identity built around visual storytelling.

His journey began during his high school years, when he developed an interest in capturing moments through photography. What started as a simple curiosity gradually grew into a passion, leading him to explore videography and further develop his skills in video editing and visual storytelling.

Through uziworks, John Michael aims to transform moments and emotions into meaningful visual stories, capturing not only what is seen, but also the feelings and stories behind every frame.
